# 📊 Revenue Analysis Dashboard — Project Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This project presents an interactive **Revenue Analysis Dashboard** designed to monitor, evaluate, and optimize e-commerce and retail financial performance. Built with a clean, executive-ready user interface, the dashboard tracks top-line KPIs, customer purchasing behavior, regional revenue distribution, product performance, and daily revenue trends over a 30-day cycle.

---

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
At a glance, the dashboard surfaces critical business health metrics:
* **Total (Net) Revenue:** `263M`
* **Gross Revenue:** `288M`
* **Total Discounts:** `14M`
* **Total Orders:** `2K`
* **Cancellation Rate:** `5%` *(Highlighted as a risk/alert metric)*
* **Revenue Per Customer:** `325K`
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** `175K`

---

## 3. Data Insights & Analytical Breakdown

### 💳 Payment Method Performance
* **Card payments** lead significantly, generating **106M** (~40.3% of total net revenue).
* **Bank Transfers** follow at **68M**, while **Cash on Delivery (57M)** and **Digital Wallets (31M)** account for the remainder.
* *Takeaway:* Digital payment adoption (Card + Bank Transfer) drives over 66% of revenue, indicating strong customer trust in electronic checkout systems.

### 👥 Customer Acquisition vs. Retention
* **New Customers:** **163M (62%)**
* **Returning Customers:** **100M (38%)**
* *Takeaway:* Customer acquisition is performing strongly, bringing in the majority of revenue. However, there is a clear opportunity to boost customer lifetime value (CLV) through targeted retention and loyalty campaigns.

### 📦 Product & Category Performance
* **Top Categories:** **Phones & Accessories** dominate revenue at **125M**, followed by **Computing (54M)** and **Home & Kitchen (34M)**. Fashion (19M), Electronics (18M), Beauty (8M), and Groceries (5M) make up the long tail.
* **Top-Performing Products:** 
  1. iPhone (`33.6M`)
  2. Android Smartphones (`32.8M`)
  3. Phone Cases (`30.1M`)
  4. Tablets (`28.2M`)
* *Takeaway:* Mobile devices and accessories represent the core revenue engine. Bundling high-margin accessories (like phone cases) with smartphones can further lift AOV.

### 🌍 Geographic Distribution (Top Cities)
* **Lagos** is the undisputed commercial hub, generating **56M**—nearly double the runner-up cities.
* **Secondary Tier Markets:** **Benin City (29M)**, **Abuja (29M)**, **Port Harcourt (29M)**, and **Enugu (27M)** show remarkably balanced, strong demand.
* **Emerging Markets:** Ibadan (23M), Kano (23M), Ilorin (18M), Kaduna (15M), and Abeokuta (15M) represent steady regional traction.

### 📈 Daily Revenue Trends (Gross vs. Net)
* The 30-day trend line shows cyclical peaks (notably around Days 3, 17, 24, and 29, reaching up to ~14M in gross daily revenue) and predictable dips (Days 8, 15, and 18).
* The tight tracking between Gross Revenue and Net Revenue confirms that discounting (`14M` total) and cancellations (`5%`) remain well-controlled without severely eroding margins.

---

## 4. Dashboard Features & UX Design
* **Interactive Slicers:** Users can dynamically filter the entire report by **Payment Method** (Bank Transfer, Card, Cash on Delivery, Wallet), **Gender**, and **Product Category**, supported by a quick **"Clear all slicers"** reset button.
* **Visual Hierarchy:** High-contrast deep purple cards draw immediate attention to primary metrics (Total Revenue, Revenue Per Customer, and the Daily Trend chart), while soft neutral backgrounds and subtle sparklines provide context without visual clutter.

---

## 5. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Launch a Retention & Loyalty Program:** With 62% of revenue coming from new buyers, introducing post-purchase incentives or loyalty points can convert first-time buyers into repeat customers.
2. **Optimize Logistics in Tier-2 Cities:** Since Benin City, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Enugu generate nearly identical revenue (~27M–29M each), targeted regional marketing and faster delivery SLAs could unlock rapid growth outside Lagos.
3. **Capitalize on Peak Sales Days:** Align flash sales, email campaigns, and ad spend with historical high-performing days (such as early-month and mid-to-late-month pay cycles).
